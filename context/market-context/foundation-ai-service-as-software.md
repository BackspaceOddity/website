# AI Leads a Service as Software Paradigm Shift

**Published:** Apr 21, 2026, 3:05 AM UTC
**Source:** Foundation Capital [Perspectives]

---

## Core Thesis

AI is catalyzing a fundamental shift from "Software as a Service" (SaaS) to "Service as Software" — where AI agents assume responsibility for outcomes rather than merely providing tools. This transition represents a **$4.6 trillion market opportunity**, dwarfing traditional software markets.

---

## Key Distinction: SaaS vs. Service as Software

**Traditional SaaS Model:**
"A company may sell access to its platform or tool, but customers are still responsible for using that tool to achieve the desired outcome."

**Service as Software Model:**
"Responsibility for achieving the desired outcome sits with the company selling the service. Instead of QuickBooks, you offer tax services — in this case, conducted by an AI accountant."

---

## The $4.6 Trillion Opportunity Breakdown

Two market buckets:

1. **Global salaries in key functions:** $2.3 trillion
   - Sales & marketing
   - Software engineering
   - Security
   - HR

2. **Outsourced services & salaries:** $2.3 trillion
   - IT services (projected $2.3 trillion by 2028; currently $1.5 trillion)
   - Business process services (forecast $303 billion by 2027)

---

## Market Context

- **SaaS growth:** From 15 software unicorns a decade ago to 416 today
- **Enterprise software market cap:** Exceeds $1 trillion
- **Salesforce annual revenue:** $35 billion (contrasted against $1.1 trillion in global sales/marketing salaries)
- **India IT services exports:** $240 billion annually

---

## Applications Across Functions

### Sales & Marketing
Portfolio company **Wizia** builds an "AI SDR" handling the complete outbound workflow: "sourcing contacts, validating info, researching target accounts, writing highly personalized sequences."

Business model shift: charging based on qualified opportunities or signed customers rather than per-seat pricing.

### Software Engineering
- 30+ million software engineers globally
- ~$1 trillion in combined salaries
- Example: **Cursor** provides AI-assisted code review, design automation, and documentation authoring

### Cybersecurity
- Industry suffers chronic understaffing
- Security analysts overwhelmed by alerts
- **AirMDR** (portfolio company) provides virtual analysts at reduced costs for SMBs

### Business Process Services
Examples of fully automated functions:
- Recruiting calls (**ConverzAI**)
- Customer inquiries (**Docket**)
- Data entry and fax processing (**Tennr**)

Gartner projects AI/generative AI technologies will inject "$28 billion into the business process services market in the next four years."

---

## Pricing & Economics Innovation

Outcome-based pricing creates advantages:

- Aligns vendor costs with actual business value delivered
- Eliminates per-seat limitations
- Scales with customer growth
- Vendors incentivized for increased usage (supporting better training data)

---

## Human-in-the-Loop Reality

Coexistence models: "Some customer segments may choose functions to outsource, while others will keep those functions in-house."

Even in routine operations like invoice processing, "they perform spot checks and look in on problem cases."
