# Services: The New Software

**Author:** Julien Bek
**Published:** March 5, 2026
**Source:** Sequoia Capital

---

## Core Thesis

The next trillion-dollar company will be "a software company masquerading as a services firm." Rather than selling tools vulnerable to displacement by improved AI models, winning companies will sell outcomes — automating work rather than augmenting workers.

---

## Intelligence vs. Judgement Framework

Foundational distinction:

- **Intelligence:** Rule-based work (coding, testing, debugging, translating specs)
- **Judgement:** Experience-driven decisions (feature prioritization, technical debt management, shipping timing)

AI has crossed a threshold where it handles most intelligence work autonomously, leaving judgement to humans. Software engineering reached this inflection first; other professions follow. "Software engineering accounts for over half of all AI tool usage" while other categories remain "in single digits."

---

## Copilots vs. Autopilots

**Copilots** = tool-based models (Harvey for law firms, Rogo for investment banks)
- Professional remains customer
- Professional retains responsibility for output

**Autopilots** = outcome-based models (Crosby for NDAs, WithCoverage for insurance)
- End customer buys direct result
- Work budgets dwarf tool budgets

The distinction matters: "the work budget in any profession dwarfs the tool budget, and autopilots capture the work budget from day one."

---

## The Convergence Thesis

As AI accumulates proprietary domain data, "today's judgement will become tomorrow's intelligence." Copilots and autopilots will eventually merge, but starting position determines which can capture customers and data now.

---

## The Autopilot Playbook

**Core principle:** "For every dollar spent on software, six are spent on services."

**Strategy:** Start where outsourcing already exists
1. Task already outsourced = company accepts external work
2. Budget line exists for substitution
3. Buyer already purchases outcomes

The wedge is outsourced intelligence work; long-term TAM is insourced judgement work. "Replacing an outsourcing contract with an AI-native services provider is a vendor swap. Replacing headcount is a reorg."

**Case study:** Crosby began with NDAs — well-defined, primarily intelligence, routinely outsourced, immediate ROI.

## Opportunity Map by Vertical

### Insurance Brokerage ($140-200B)
- Standard commercial lines heavily standardized
- Broker value = shopping carriers + form completion (intelligence)
- Fragmented distribution (thousands of small brokers)
- Players: WithCoverage, Harper

### Accounting & Audit ($50-80B outsourced in US)
- 340,000 accountant loss over five years
- 75% of CPAs nearing retirement
- Structural shortage accelerates AI adoption
- Players: Rillet (AI-native ERP), Basis (copilot)

### Healthcare Revenue Cycle ($50-80B outsourced)
- Billing layer nearly pure intelligence
- Medical coding = translating notes to ~70,000 ICD-10 codes
- Outcome-based outsourcing already mature
- Player: Anterior

### Claims Adjusting ($50-80B including TPAs)
- Interpreting policy language against damage schedules
- Adjuster workforce aging out
- Highly outsourced to independents and TPAs
- Players: Pace, Strala

### Tax Advisory ($30-35B)
- 80-90% underlying work is intelligence
- Multi-jurisdiction complexity outsourced by SMBs
- Regulatory moat (CPA licensing) creates data advantage
- Players: TaxGPT, Skalar, Ravical

### Legal/Transactional ($20-25B)
- Contract drafting, NDAs, regulatory filings
- Work product standardized enough for verification
- Players: Harvey (moving to autopilot), Crosby, Lawhive

### IT Managed Services ($100B+)
- Every SMB outsources IT
- Patching, monitoring, provisioning, alert triage (intelligence)
- No incumbent sells direct outcomes to companies
- Players: Edra, Serval

### Supply Chain & Procurement ($200B+)
- Enterprises negotiate seriously with only top 20% suppliers
- Long tail receives zero attention (uneconomical)
- Contract leakage: 2-5% of total spend
- Wedge: abandoned work (no budget line, no incumbent)
- Players: Magentic (direct), AskLio (indirect), Tacto

### Recruitment & Staffing ($200B+)
- Largest services market
- Top funnel (screening, matching, outreach) = intelligence
- Candidate closing + culture fit = judgement
- Autopilot wedge: high-volume, low-judgement roles
- Players: Juicebox, Mercor, Jack & Jill

### Management Consulting ($300-400B)
- Mostly judgement-heavy work
- Potential disaggregation: intelligence (data gathering, benchmarking) vs. judgement (strategy)
- Best candidates "TBD"

## Market Dynamics & Timing

2025 saw fastest-growing AI companies as copilots. In 2026, many attempt autopilot transitions but face the "innovator's dilemma: selling the work means cutting their own customers out of doing it." This creates opportunity for pure-play autopilots.

---

## Key Data Points

- Six dollars spent on services for every dollar spent on software
- 340,000 accountants lost in five years (US)
- 75% of CPAs nearing retirement
- ~70,000 ICD-10 medical coding standards
- 2-5% contract leakage in procurement spend
- Healthcare coding tasks already outsourced and outcome-based
